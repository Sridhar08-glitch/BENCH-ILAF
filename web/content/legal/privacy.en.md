## 2. What we collect

- **Your account.** Your name, e-mail address, a protected (hashed) password or your Google sign-in identifier, your language setting and the date you joined.
- **Your cards.** What you put on a card: name, job title, company, photo, buttons and links, design choices and the card address.
- **Your company workspace.** The company name, logo and colours, the team members you invite (name, e-mail address, role, department), the field rules and approvals, and a log of the changes owners and admins make.
- **Contacts and requests from visitors.** When someone shares their details with a card owner or asks for a meeting, we store what they typed (name, and any of e-mail address, phone number, company and message), the time, how they reached the card, and that they agreed to share their details.
- **Visitor statistics.** When a card is opened we count a visit, button taps, saves and shares. We keep the country, the way the card was reached (for example QR code or link) and a one-way daily code that lets us count unique visitors. We do not store the visitor's IP address with these statistics and we do not build profiles of people.
- **Security records.** Your IP address is used to limit abuse and is kept only as a one-way code in the company activity log. Technical logs of the servers hold addresses for a short time.
- **Billing.** Plan, seats, invoice numbers and amounts. When card payments are switched on, the payment itself is handled by our payment provider and we never see your card number.
- **E-mails we send.** Sign-in and password messages, invitations, contact alerts and the weekly report you choose to receive.

## 3. Why we use it, and on what basis

- To provide the service you asked for: accounts, cards, contacts, teams, statistics and e-mails (performance of the contract).
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interests).
- To meet legal duties such as accounting and answering lawful requests (legal obligation).
- To send the weekly report and similar messages, which you can switch off in Settings (your choice).

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Cookies and similar tools

{{APP_NAME}} uses only the cookies it needs to work. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, we do not load tracking scripts, and the fonts on card pages are served from our own server, not from a third party. For this reason we do not show a cookie banner.

- **Sign-in cookies** (two, for signed-in people only): they keep you signed in and are not readable by page scripts. They end when you sign out or after 30 days at most.
- **Language and number-style cookies**: they remember the language you chose. They last up to one year.
- **Browser storage**: your browser remembers which workspace you last opened and a draft preview of a card while you edit it. This stays on your device.

If you press "Continue with Google", Google's sign-in script is loaded at that moment and Google may set its own cookies under its own policy.

## 7. How long we keep data

- Visit statistics: the detailed records are kept for 400 days. After that they are summed into totals that no longer identify a visitor.
- Contacts and cards: until you or your company delete them, or the account is closed.
- Deleted accounts and companies: after you ask for deletion there is a 14-day period in which you can change your mind and restore the account. After that the data is erased for good.
- Data export files: available for 24 hours, then deleted.
- Webhook delivery logs: 90 days.
- Company activity log: kept while the company exists.
- Backups: kept for [BACKUP PERIOD] and then overwritten.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or limit how we use it, and to withdraw a consent you gave.

- **Export:** in the app, go to Settings, then Privacy and data, and choose "Export my data". Company owners can also export the company data.
- **Delete:** in the same place you can delete your account or your company.
- **Anything else:** write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We answer within 30 days.

If you left your details on someone's card, ask that card owner first: they decide what happens to your contact details. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

## 9. Security

Passwords are stored hashed, traffic is encrypted, access to data is limited by role, uploads are checked, and sign-ins that are no longer needed can be ended from Settings (sign out of all devices). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.
