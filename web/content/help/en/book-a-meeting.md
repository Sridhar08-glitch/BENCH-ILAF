Open your card, go to the Buttons tab and switch on Book a meeting. You have two options.

## My booking link
Paste the page where people book time with you: Calendly, a Google Calendar appointment page, Cal.com, Microsoft Bookings or any link that starts with https://. If it is recognised, your card shows its logo and colour. Use Test link to check it.

## The built-in form
Without a link, visitors pick up to three times that suit them and leave their name, email and a message. You get an email, and a contact marked Meeting request appears in [Contacts](/app/contacts).

> Use a link that starts with https:// and the full web address of your booking page, for example https://calendly.com/yourname.
