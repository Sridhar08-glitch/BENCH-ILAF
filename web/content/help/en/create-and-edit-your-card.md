## Make a card
Open Cards and press New card. It starts as a draft with your name filled in. Changes save as you type, and the pill at the top shows when everything is saved.

## Profile tab
Add your full name, job title, company and address. Upload a square photo (at least the size shown under the upload button). Under Card address you can choose a short address of 3 to 40 letters, numbers or dashes. Old links keep working after you change it.

You can also choose the card language, hide yourself from the company directory, and decide what is saved to a visitor's phone when they press Save contact.

## Buttons tab
Switch on a platform, type your number or link, and use the arrows to put the most important button first. Search finds many brands, and Add any link takes any web address, email or phone number. Switching a button off hides it without deleting what you typed.

> In a company, some buttons may be required, and some kinds or websites may not be allowed. The editor tells you why.

## The preview
The preview beside the editor is your card exactly as visitors see it. On a phone, use the Preview button.
