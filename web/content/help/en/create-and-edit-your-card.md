## Make a card
Open Cards and press New card. It starts as a draft with your name filled in. Changes save as you type, and the pill at the top shows when everything is saved.

## The steps
A draft card opens step by step: **Look, Colour, Details, Buttons, Under the card, Extras, More settings, Share.** The step list at the top lets you jump to any of them. A page style has no "Under the card" step, and a company that locks the design hides the look steps.

- **Details.** Only what your design shows is asked: name, job title, company, address, intro text, photo and cover picture. Pick another look and the list changes.
- **Buttons.** Switch on a platform, type your number or link, and use the arrows to put the most important button first. Each button appears once on your card. Switching a button off hides it without deleting what you typed.
- **Extras.** Video, shop, opening hours, booking, team, gallery, event and map, shown under the card. In a company, you only see the extras your company lets you add. If it allows none, this step and the Orders and Bookings pages are hidden.
- **More settings.** Your card address (3 to 40 letters, numbers or dashes), the card language, the company directory and what is saved to a visitor's phone when they press Save contact.
- **Share.** Publish, copy the link, get the QR code and add the card to a wallet.

> In a company, some buttons may be required, and some kinds or websites may not be allowed. The editor tells you why.

## The preview
The preview beside the editor is your card as visitors see it, with its extras under it. The video plays and the shop and booking open, but the order and booking forms send nothing from the preview. On a phone, use the Preview button.

## Orders and bookings
They belong to the card they came in on. You see the orders and bookings of your own cards, and no one else's: an admin does not see an employee's, and an employee does not see the company card's.
