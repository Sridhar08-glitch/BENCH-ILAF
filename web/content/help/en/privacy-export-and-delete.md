Open Settings, then Account, then Privacy and data.

## Export my data
You get a ZIP file with your profile, your cards, the contacts left on them, simple visit totals, your activity records and your pictures. We email you a link that works once and expires, and it also appears on the page. You can ask for up to three exports a day.

Company owners can also export the company: team, cards, contacts and the activity log.

## Delete my account
Your account switches off at once and your cards go offline. For 14 days you can change your mind: sign in and choose Restore my account. After that your profile, cards, pictures and sign-in are erased. Contacts left on cards you made for a company stay with that company.

## Delete a company
Only the owner can do this. Cards go offline and the team is locked out for 14 days, during which you can restore the company. Export the company data first if you want a copy.

> For your safety we may ask for your password, or a fresh Google sign-in, before these changes.
