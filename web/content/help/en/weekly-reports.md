Open Settings, then Reports and integrations, then Weekly report.

- **My cards:** views, QR scans, saves, button clicks and new contacts of your own cards, with the change from the week before.
- **The whole company** (owners and admins): totals, the five best people, contacts by status, contacts nobody owns yet and changes waiting for approval.

Switch each report on or off. Every email also has an Unsubscribe link that works without signing in.

## Preview
Show preview displays what the email looks like with your real numbers. Choose last full week or this week so far. Nothing is sent.
