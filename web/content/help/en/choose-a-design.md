In your card, the Look step is the first. At the top, choose **Card faces** (500 designs) or **Page styles** (headers with their own look). A page style comes with its own buttons layout, so there is no separate layout step for it.

1. **Face.** The front of your card. Search by name or number, or filter by style such as corporate, luxury or minimal.
2. **Colours.** Every face has four colour sets made for it. In a company with brand colours, the company colours appear too.
3. **Text.** Pick a font and a text size from 60 to 150 percent. Some faces have lettering that is part of the look and keep their own fonts.
4. **Below the card.** How your buttons and details are laid out under the card (card faces only).
5. **Opening.** The animation shown once when someone opens your card. Card faces have 400 to choose from. Page styles have six made for them. Press Play to see it.
6. **Finish.** Only the switches your design has: show your photo or your initials, keep each button's own brand colour, show or hide the Powered by line. A cover picture is asked for in Details, for designs that use one.

> If your company has locked the design, you see "set by your company" and can still change your details, buttons and extras.
