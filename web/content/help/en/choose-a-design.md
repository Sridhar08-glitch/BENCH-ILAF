Open your card and go to the Design tab. It is a short walk through six steps.

1. **Face.** The front of your card. Search by name or number, or filter by style such as corporate, luxury or minimal.
2. **Colours.** Every face has four colour sets made for it. In a company with brand colours, the company colours appear too.
3. **Text.** Pick a font and a text size from 60 to 150 percent. Some faces have lettering that is part of the look and keep their own fonts. The editor tells you the largest size that fits without overlapping.
4. **Below the card.** How your buttons and details are laid out under the card.
5. **Animation.** The opening shown once when someone opens your card. Press Play to see it.
6. **Finish.** Show your photo or your initials, keep each button's own brand colour, show or hide the Powered by line, and add a cover picture where the face uses one.

> Reset design puts everything back. If your company has locked the design, you see a notice and can still change your details and buttons.
