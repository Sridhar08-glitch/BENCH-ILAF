## The logo looks wrong
- **No logo yet.** Without a logo the cards show a monogram made from your company name. Add one in the Company step.
- **Hard to see.** In the Logo step set the chip behind it. Automatic puts the logo on a white or dark chip only where it would be hard to read.
- **In the wrong place.** Change the placement and the corner in the Logo step. Automatic corner mode uses the corner no text or photo needs.
- **Too small or blurry.** Upload a larger picture. PNG, JPG or WebP work, and a logo with a transparent background looks best.

## Colours are hard to read
Your two brand colours are turned into a five-colour palette that keeps text readable. If your accent was too close to the card colour we move its lightness a little, and tell you. Try Light or Dark card mode if a colour is too strong as a background.

## Text on artwork
With a background or template, open the readability check. A busy spot gets a soft panel behind the text, and zone D draws a solid band. Try another zone from A to D.

> Preview your sample employee card after each change, and open it in a new tab to see it full size.
