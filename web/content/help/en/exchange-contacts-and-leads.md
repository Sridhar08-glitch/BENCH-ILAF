## Let visitors share their details
In the card editor, turn on Let visitors share their details. A Share your details button appears under your card. Their name is always asked. Choose whether to ask for email, phone and a message, and add an optional consent sentence.

## Contacts
What visitors send arrives in Contacts, and by email. Contacts also holds meeting requests and cards you scanned.

- **Who sees what:** you see the contacts that came in on your own cards, and any that were assigned to you. Owners and admins also see the contacts of the company's own card, and the ones an employee chose to share (see below). Nobody sees the other contacts of someone else's card, an owner or admin included.
- **Tabs:** All, Mine, Given to me and Unassigned.
- **Filters:** search by name, email or company, and filter by status, card, source or dates.
- **Status:** New, Contacted, Qualified, Won or Lost. Open a contact to change it and add notes.
- **Assign:** tick contacts and choose Assign to. The colleague gets one email.
- **Export:** you can export what you see as a CSV file that opens in Excel.
- **Add contact:** the Add contact menu on the Contacts page has Scan a paper card, which saves a business card you photograph.

> Contacts collected on the company's own card always stay with the company. The same goes for webhooks, CRM connectors, the developer API and the company data export: they carry the company's own contacts and the ones an employee shared, never the rest of an employee's.
>
> Company admins can ask to be emailed about the company card's contacts and the ones an employee shared. The other contacts of an employee are emailed to that employee only.

## Share my new contacts with the company (employees)
If you are an employee, your contacts, orders and bookings are yours alone. In Settings you can turn on Share my new contacts with the company. It is off until you turn it on.

- **From that moment**, what arrives on your card is also the company's. Owners and admins can see it, and it reaches the company's CRM connections, webhooks, reports and exports.
- **What arrived before stays private.** Turning it on never reaches back.
- **Turning it off** stops sharing for what arrives after that. What you already shared stays with the company, because it was given to it.
- **You can change it at any time.** Contacts you shared show a Shared with company badge. Owners and admins see on the Team page whether you share or keep your contacts private, but only you can change it.
