## Let visitors share their details
In the card editor, turn on Let visitors share their details. A Share your details button appears under your card. Their name is always asked. Choose whether to ask for email, phone and a message, and add an optional consent sentence.

## Contacts
What visitors send arrives in Contacts, and by email. Contacts also holds meeting requests and cards you scanned.

- **Tabs:** All, Mine, Given to me and Unassigned (owners and admins see all).
- **Filters:** search by name, email or company, and filter by status, card, source or dates.
- **Status:** New, Contacted, Qualified, Won or Lost. Open a contact to change it and add notes.
- **Assign:** tick contacts and choose Assign to. The colleague gets one email.
- **Export:** owners and admins can export what they see as a CSV file that opens in Excel.

> Contacts collected on a company card always stay with the company, even if the person leaves.
