## My card is not visible
- **Is it published?** A draft is only visible to you. Look at the status pill in the editor or on the card list, and press Publish.
- **Suspended?** A company admin may have suspended it. Ask them to restore it.
- **Plan ended?** When a plan ends, cards stay online for a grace period and then go offline. An owner can renew under Plan and billing.
- **Link changed?** Old links keep working after you change the address, but check you are using the link from the QR page.

## The invite did not arrive
- Check the spam or junk folder, and that the address is typed correctly.
- On the Team page, press Resend invite on the person's row. It sends a fresh link. If it says to wait, try again after a few minutes.
- If your company only allows certain email domains, the address must be one of them.
- If there are no free seats, you cannot invite more people until you buy seats or free one.

> The invitation link works for a limited time. Resend it if it has expired.
