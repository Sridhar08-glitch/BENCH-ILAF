## Publish
A card is a draft until you publish it. Use Publish in the editor or on the card list. You can unpublish at any time and the link then shows a paused page.

## QR and share
Open QR and share to find everything in one place.

- **Link.** Copy it, or send it by WhatsApp or email. Links shared by email can carry a tag so you can see where visits come from.
- **QR code.** Download it as PNG or SVG. Print it at least 4 cm wide.
- **Contact inside the QR.** A second code holds your contact details, so a phone can save you even with no internet. It cannot show your card.
- **NFC link.** Write it to an NFC tag or card. {{APP}} counts taps separately from scans.

> If the card is not published yet, the QR page tells you, because people who scan it will not see anything.

More ways: your [email signature](/app/help/email-signature), a [call background](/app/help/call-background), and Save to wallet on the card editor where it is offered.
