Open Share and choose the Email signature tab.

## Make it
1. Pick a style: Compact, Classic with photo, Banner with logo or Minimal text.
2. Choose options: your photo, your QR code, a View my card button and up to three extra buttons.
3. Choose the colours: your card colours, the company colours or the standard ink and orange.
4. Check the preview.

## Add it to your mail app
Press Copy signature, then:

- **Gmail:** Settings, See all settings, Signature, Create new, paste, Save changes.
- **Outlook:** Settings, Mail, Compose and reply (or File, Options, Mail, Signatures on Windows), New, paste, Save.
- **Apple Mail:** Settings, Signatures, the plus button, untick Always match my default message font, paste.

> If your mail app does not accept the pasted design, use Copy HTML source or Copy plain text.
