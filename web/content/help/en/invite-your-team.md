Open Team. Only owners and admins manage the team.

## Invite people
1. Type or paste email addresses, one per line. You can add a name and title on the same line: omar@company.com, Omar Ali, Sales.
2. Choose a role and, if you use departments, a department.
3. Press Send invitations.

Each person gets an email with a link to join and a draft card in your company design.

## Import a list
Press Import CSV. The columns are email, full_name, title and, optionally, department (an existing department name). People added from a CSV join as employees.

## Seats
Seats used shows how many seats you have taken. Invited, active and suspended people each take one. If there are no free seats left, buy more under Plan and billing.

> If someone did not get the email, use Resend invite on their row. It sends a fresh link, and you can send it again after a short wait.
