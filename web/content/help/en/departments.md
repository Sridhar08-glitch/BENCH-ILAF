Open Departments. Use New department to create one, for example Sales or Support.

## What a department can do
- **Its own design.** A department can have its own face, colours, layout and opening.
- **Required buttons.** Every card in the department keeps these buttons, on top of the company-wide ones.
- **Filtering.** Filter the Team page and the analytics by department.

## Put people in a department
Choose the department when you invite someone, change it from their row on the Team page, or select several people and use the bulk department action.

> With the design lock on, a department's own look only applies if you tick Let departments keep their own look on the Brand page.

Deleting a department does not delete its people or change their cards.
