Open Analytics.

## Sections
- **Overview:** totals, the trend, top countries, devices and top buttons.
- **People** and **Departments:** who gets the views, for owners and admins.
- **Links:** which buttons get tapped.
- **Sources:** how people arrived: QR scans, NFC taps, links and email.

## Time range
Choose 7, 30 or 90 days, or pick your own dates and press Apply. Days follow your time zone. Each number is compared with the period before. Group by days, weeks or months where it is offered.

## Export
Export table takes the table you are looking at to a spreadsheet.

> A card you have not published yet has no views. Analytics starts counting when people open it.
