{{APP}} gives you a digital business card with its own link and QR code. Your checklist on the home page walks you through it.

## The five steps
1. **Add your name and photo.** Open Cards, choose New card, and fill in the Profile tab.
2. **Add your buttons.** In the Buttons tab switch on phone, WhatsApp, email, website or social links.
3. **Choose a design.** In the Design tab pick a face, colours and the layout below the card.
4. **Publish your card.** A new card is a draft that only you can see. Press Publish when you are happy.
5. **Share your QR code.** Open Share to show the code, copy your link or download the picture.

> You can leave and come back at any time. Everything is saved as you type, and the checklist ticks steps off by itself.

## After that
Add your card to your [email signature](/app/help/email-signature), or [scan a paper card](/app/help/scan-a-paper-card) into your contacts.

Working with colleagues? When your card shows a company, the home page may suggest turning this into a team. Company plans start at a minimum number of seats and have no free week; you choose a plan on the billing page.
