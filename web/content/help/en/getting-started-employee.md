Your company has already made a card for you in the company design.

## What to do
1. **Open the invitation email** and follow its link. Choose a password, or sign in with Google if your company allows it.
2. **Fill in your details.** Open My card and add your job title, your photo and the buttons people should tap.
3. **Send changes for approval**, if your company checks some fields. You will see a notice that the change is waiting, and you get an email when it is decided.
4. **Publish your card** when it is ready.
5. **Share your QR code** from the Share page.
6. **Decide about your contacts.** The contacts, orders and bookings that arrive on your card are yours alone. In Settings you can turn on Share my new contacts with the company; it is off until you do, and you can change it at any time. See [exchange contacts and leads](/app/help/exchange-contacts-and-leads).

> Your company may lock some fields or the whole design. A locked field shows a short note saying who sets it.

If the email never arrived, see [invite not received](/app/help/troubleshooting-card-and-invites).
