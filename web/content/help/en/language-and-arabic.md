## The app
Use the language picker in the sidebar (on a phone, at the top) to switch between English and Arabic. Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to other devices and sets the language of the emails you receive. Arabic reads from right to left.

In Settings, Language and numbers also lets you choose Western or Arabic-Indic digits.

## Your card
In the Profile tab, Card language decides the language and direction of your public page: English, Arabic, or Automatic, which follows each visitor's own language. Names and details written in Arabic work in any language.

> You can write your name and job title in Arabic or English, and keep both by choosing Automatic and adding the details in the language you prefer.
