A company workspace gives every employee a card in one design. Follow the checklist on the home page in this order.

## The steps
1. **Add your logo and brand colours** on the Brand page.
2. **Choose the card style** for the whole company in the same page.
3. **Lock the design for everyone** in the last step, so employees keep their own details but the look stays yours.
4. **Invite your team** on the Team page. Each person gets an email and a draft card.
5. **Set up departments** if Sales, Support and others should start with their own look and buttons.
6. **Publish the first cards**, starting with your own.
7. **Turn on the company directory** (optional, owner only).

> You need an active company plan to invite people. Each invited person takes one seat until they are removed.

## Good to know
Decide what employees may change before you invite many people: see [field rules and approvals](/app/help/field-rules-and-approvals).
