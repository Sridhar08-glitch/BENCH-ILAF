Open Settings, then Account, then Sign-in methods.

## Google
Connect your Google account to sign in with one tap. Your password keeps working. If Google is your only way in, set a password first before you disconnect it.

## Password
Change your password in Settings. If you signed up with Google only, Set a password gives you a second way in.

## Sign out of all devices
Ends every sign-in of your account on every phone and computer, including this one. Use it if a device was lost or you shared your account. A device that is signed in may keep working for up to an hour.

## Company sign-in rules
The owner of a company can limit invites to certain email domains and require Google sign-in for employees. Owners are not affected.

> Not sure which address you used? Use Forgot password on the sign-in page.
