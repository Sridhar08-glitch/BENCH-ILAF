Open Contacts, choose Add contact, then Scan a paper card.

## Read the card
Take a photo or choose a picture of the paper card. English and Arabic are read. The photo is read on your device and is never uploaded. Lay the card flat in good light so it fills the picture.

If the reader is not available, or does not find text, paste the text of the card instead.

## Check and save
We show what was found with a note beside each field: Looks right, Check this or Not found. Fix anything that is wrong, then choose:

- **Save to my contacts as vCard.** It opens in your phone or computer contacts.
- **Create a lead from it.** It goes to your Contacts list, marked Scanned card.
- **Fill my card.** Copy details onto your own card. It shows what will change and asks first.

> Every contact needs a name. Add one if the reader missed it.
