Open Background in the Tools section.

## Make it
1. Choose a layout: QR on the left, QR on the right, Centred, Corner, Banner or Minimal.
2. Choose the colours: your card colours, dark or light.
3. Turn on Soft background if your video app blurs it, and Show my company logo if there is room for it.
4. Press Download PNG.

The Show the place where I sit option draws a guide on the preview only. It is not in the picture.

## Use it
- **Zoom:** Settings, Backgrounds and effects, then the plus button.
- **Teams:** Effects and avatars, Video effects, Add new.
- **Google Meet:** Apply visual effects, then add your own picture.

> The picture is 1920 by 1080 pixels. Keep your face clear of the QR code, so people can scan it.
