Open Directory. Only the owner can switch it on or off and change its address.

1. Turn on Show the directory to the public.
2. Choose a directory address.
3. Share the link or the QR code that appears.

Only published cards are listed, and the page tells you how many of them appear. Anyone can hide their own card from the directory in the Profile tab of their card. The card stays live for anyone with its link.

> The directory is optional. You can turn it off again at any time.
