The Brand page is a guided flow of six steps. You can jump to any step, and Next saves the step you are on.

1. **Company.** Name, website and logo.
2. **Colours.** Two brand colours become a full, readable card palette of five colours. Choose whether cards are light, dark or in the brand colours.
3. **Logo.** How the logo shows: display, placement, corner and the chip behind it.
4. **Artwork.** Choose Colours and logo, Background, Logo pattern or Company template. A custom face designed for you is also offered.
5. **Card style.** The face, colours, layout below the card and opening for the whole company.
6. **Finish.** Rules for the team and a last look at every choice.

## Lock the design
In the last step, Lock the design for everyone makes every card use the company design. You can let departments keep their own look while the lock is on.
