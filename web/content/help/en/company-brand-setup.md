The Brand page is a guided flow of five steps, in the same order as a personal card: the look first, then the details. You can jump to any step, and Next saves the step you are on.

1. **Look.** Choose card faces or page styles with the two buttons at the top. Page styles are headers with their own look and their own buttons below.
2. **Company details.** The name on cards, the logo and the symbol, and the brand colours. Two colours become a full, readable card palette of five colours. A button suggests colours from your logo. You can keep the text colour automatic, or choose your own: a warning shows when it is hard to read.
3. **Logo.** Which picture is shown (automatic, full logo, symbol, symbol and name), where (on the card, above it, or both), the corner, the backing behind it, and its **size** from 50 to 150 percent. Under **Position** choose Automatic, or Free: drag the logo on the preview to the spot you want. A free spot belongs to the look you are on only; other looks stay automatic.
4. **Your team.** One choice for what people may change on their own cards (anything, changes need approval, or nothing), and one for which **extras** they may add (any, only some, or none). Then invite people.
5. **More settings.** Workspace name, website and custom domain; the artwork (background, logo pattern or company template); the design lock and the Powered by line; and a summary of every choice.

## Lock the design
In More settings, Lock the design for everyone makes every card use the company design. You can let departments keep their own look while the lock is on.

## The company's own card
On the Cards page, owners and admins can make one card for the company itself, with its own shop, hours or bookings. Only owners and admins can see and edit it, and its orders and bookings belong to the company.
