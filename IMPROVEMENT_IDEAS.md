# BENCH: ideas to make it friendlier and shorter to use

A working list of ideas. Nothing here is built unless it says so. Each idea has the benefit, a rough size
(S = under a day, M = a few days, L = a week or more) and the first step.

## A. Fewer steps to the first card

| # | Idea | Benefit | Size |
|---|------|---------|------|
| A1 | **Start from a link or a paper card.** Paste a LinkedIn or website address, or photograph a paper card; BENCH fills name, title, company and links. The paper card scan already exists, so it can be reused. | Biggest time saving at sign-up | M |
| A2 | **Card ready right after "Continue with Google".** Show a finished card with the person's name and photo; ask only for the phone number. | One screen from sign-up to a card | M |
| A3 | **Smart design defaults.** Pick a face and colour from the logo or photo. A "Surprise me" button for the rest. | People skip browsing 400 faces | S |
| A4 | **Publish in one tap,** then show every sharing option on one screen: QR, link, NFC, Wallet, e-mail signature. | Fewer clicks after publishing | S |
| A5 | **Progress guide on the dashboard** ("add a photo, add a phone, publish"), with each step one tap away. | Fewer people stop halfway | S |
| A6 | **Undo for deletes and unpublish** for a few seconds, instead of a confirmation dialog. | Less friction, fewer mistakes | S |

## B. Sharing without friction

| # | Idea | Benefit | Size |
|---|------|---------|------|
| B1 | **"Share now" full-screen page:** big QR, Add to Wallet, Copy link, NFC. The screen you open at an event. | The key moment feels easy | S |
| B2 | **Send by name or number:** type a contact and BENCH opens WhatsApp or SMS with the link ready. | One tap instead of copy and paste | S |
| B3 | **NFC guide with a test button,** and "write to a sticker" in two taps. | Fewer failed NFC setups | S |
| B4 | **Home screen shortcut and lock-screen pass** for people who do not use Wallet. | The QR is always one tap away | M |
| B5 | **Apple Wallet pass** for iPhone users (needs an Apple developer account). | Covers iPhone | L |
| B6 | **Samsung Wallet pass.** | Covers Samsung phones that do not use Google Wallet | M |
| B7 | **Share a specific link only:** QR that opens a menu, a catalogue or a booking page, not the whole card. | Useful for shops and events | M |
| B8 | **Campaign links with their own QR** that count their own visits. | Shows which event or print worked | M |

## C. After someone shares their details

| # | Idea | Benefit | Size |
|---|------|---------|------|
| C1 | **Thank-you page with "Save to my phone"** and the owner's card offered back. | Both sides end up with a contact | S |
| C2 | **One-tap follow-up message** ("Nice to meet you at [event]") from the new-contact bell. | Turns contacts into conversations | M |
| C3 | **Automatic event tag** on contacts collected in event mode. | Easy to filter later | S |
| C4 | **Duplicate merge** when the same person shares twice. | Cleaner contact list | M |
| C5 | **Reminders to follow up** after 2 or 7 days for contacts nobody answered. | More follow-ups happen | M |
| C6 | **Export to the phone's contacts or Google Contacts** in one click. | No retyping | S |
| C7 | **Meeting booking link** in the card (calendar slots), with the booking landing in the contact list. | Fewer back-and-forth messages | L |

## D. Companies and teams

| # | Idea | Benefit | Size |
|---|------|---------|------|
| D1 | **Bulk invite with a CSV file:** every person gets a card pre-filled with the company design. | Onboards 50 people in minutes | M |
| D2 | **Edit once, update everywhere:** logo, address, colours and social links change on all employee cards. | Less admin work | S |
| D3 | **When someone leaves:** one click unpublishes the card and hands their contacts to a colleague. | No lost leads | M |
| D4 | **Team leaderboard** of views, saves and contacts. | Teams use the product more | S |
| D5 | **Card templates:** the company locks the layout, and each employee fills in only their own details. | Brand stays consistent | M |
| D6 | **Departments and locations** with their own address and phone on each card. | Fits larger companies | M |
| D7 | **Approval before an employee's card goes public.** | Control for strict companies | S (partly exists) |

## E. Keeping people coming back

| # | Idea | Benefit | Size |
|---|------|---------|------|
| E1 | **Weekly summary e-mail:** views, saves, new contacts. (Needs the mail setup.) | Brings people back | M |
| E2 | **Gentle reminders** for missing details (no photo, no phone, no company). | Better cards | S |
| E3 | **Insights in plain words:** "Most people open your card on Tuesday mornings." | Feels useful, not technical | M |
| E4 | **Referral reward:** an extra month for each person who signs up. | Growth | M |
| E5 | **Yearly "your networking recap."** | Shareable and fun | M |

## F. The card itself

| # | Idea | Benefit | Size |
|---|------|---------|------|
| F1 | **Arabic and English fields on one card:** the visitor sees the matching language automatically. | Important for Qatar | M |
| F2 | **Writing help for the bio and the job title** (suggestions, not forced). | Faster setup | M |
| F3 | **Temporary cards** that expire after an event. | Privacy for one-off events | S |
| F4 | **Hide a field per visitor:** for example the phone is shown only after the visitor shares their own details. | Control over privacy | M |
| F5 | **Video or voice greeting** on the card. | Stands out | L |
| F6 | **Photo cropping and background clean-up** when uploading a photo. | Better-looking cards | M |
| F7 | **Add to calendar button** for events. | Useful for event cards | S |

## G. Easier support and onboarding

| # | Idea | Benefit | Size |
|---|------|---------|------|
| G1 | **Suggest a help article** while a person types a support message. | Fewer tickets | M |
| G2 | **Short tour on first sign-in,** skippable, in English and Arabic. | Less confusion | S |
| G3 | **Friendlier empty pages:** each empty list says what it is for and has one button. | Fewer dead ends | S |
| G4 | **Error messages that say what to do next** ("Add a company name, then press Save"). | Fewer support requests | S |
| G5 | **"Why can't I do this?" explanation** on locked features, with a one-tap upgrade. | Clearer plans | S |

## H. Quality and trust

| # | Idea | Benefit | Size |
|---|------|---------|------|
| H1 | **Accessibility pass** (screen readers, large text, contrast) on the card and the dashboard. | Reaches more people | M |
| H2 | **Offline card:** the owner's own card and QR open without internet. | Works at events with weak signal | M |
| H3 | **Speed budget** for the public card page, checked in every build. | Cards open fast on slow phones | M |
| H4 | **Privacy centre** for visitors: see what a card collected and ask for deletion. | Trust and law | M |
| H5 | **Data export** of all contacts in one click. | Trust | S (partly exists) |

## I. Connections to other tools

| # | Idea | Benefit | Size |
|---|------|---------|------|
| I1 | **Zapier or Make connector** so contacts flow into any tool. | One integration, many tools | M |
| I2 | **WhatsApp message** to the owner for each new contact, not only e-mail. | Faster reaction | M |
| I3 | **Slack or Teams alert** for company admins. | Teams see leads at once | S |
| I4 | **HubSpot, Zoho and Salesforce** connections that exist now, with a setup checker. | Fewer broken connections | S |

## Suggested order
1. A1, A3, A4, B1 (the first five minutes and the sharing moment).
2. D1, D2 (companies bring most of the revenue).
3. F1 (Arabic and English fields), since the first customers are in Qatar.
4. C1, C2 (turn contacts into follow-ups).
5. E1 once the mail is set up.

## Notes
- Mail setup is a prerequisite for E1, C5 and the e-mail parts of D3.
- Apple Wallet needs an Apple developer account (about 99 USD a year).
- Domain, payments and the real server are separate, parked topics.
