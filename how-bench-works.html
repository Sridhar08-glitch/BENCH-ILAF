How Bench works, step by step

A simple walk through making a card, running a company, and using Bench at an event or exhibition. Each step says what you do, what happens, and where to find it in the app.

The short versionYou can build a card for free. The first time you publish it, you get 7 free days. After that it needs a paid Personal plan, or it goes offline. Nothing is deleted.

  1. Sign up Ready today

    You
    Create an account with your email or with Google.
    Happens
    Bench makes your personal workspace. No card details are asked.
    Where
    Sign-in page

  2. Create your card Ready today

    You
    Press New card and type your name.
    Happens
    The card is saved as a draft. Nobody can see it yet. Creating it is free, and you can build one draft without any plan.
    Where
    Cards

  3. Make it yours Ready today

    You
    Go through the editor tabs: Look, Colour, Details, Buttons, Under the card, Extras, More settings, Share. A live preview of the card is always beside you.
    Happens
    Changes save by themselves. On the free plan you can choose from the first 50 faces, 50 below-card layouts and 50 animations, and no page styles. A paid plan opens all 500 faces and all 127 page styles.
    Where
    Cards → your card

  4. Publish Ready today

    You
    Press Publish.
    Happens
    The card goes live and your 7 free days start now, not when you created it. So take your time building.
    Where
    Card editor → Publish

  5. Share it Ready today

    You
    Show the QR code, send the link, tap an NFC card or tag, add it to your phone wallet, or put it in your email signature.
    Happens
    Anyone who scans or taps sees your card in a browser. No app is needed.
    Where
    Share (QR, NFC, email signature, wallet and Event mode in one place)

  6. People meet your card Ready today

    They
    Save your contact, or send you their own details.
    Happens
    Their details arrive in your Contacts and you get an email alert. Only you can see them. You can also add a contact by scanning a paper business card.
    Where
    Contacts (Add contact → Scan a paper card)

  7. Two days before the free week ends Ready today

    Happens
    You get an email in English or Arabic: your free week is about to end.
    Where
    Your inbox, and a banner in the app with a countdown

  8. Day 7: you choose Partly verified

    You pay the Personal plan

    The card stays online. All designs unlock. It renews each year until you cancel, and you can manage that under Billing. The paid year starts on the day you pay.

    You do not pay

    The card goes offline straight away, with no extra grace days. Visitors see "temporarily unavailable". Nothing is deleted, but you cannot publish again until you pay. There is no second free week.
    Where
    Plan and billing

    The rules are tested. A real payment has not been tried yet, because no payment keys are set up here.

The short versionA company gets one card per person, all in the company's look. The owner and admins manage the team. Each employee's own contacts stay private unless that employee chooses to share them.

  1. Create the company Ready today

    You
    Open the workspace menu and add a company. A personal user with a work email also sees a gentle "Turn this into a team" prompt.
    Happens
    You become the owner. Bench makes a company workspace next to your personal one.
    Where
    Workspace menu → New company

  2. Choose a plan Ready today

    Company
    39 QAR per seat per year, from 5 seats. Renews yearly. There is no free week for companies.
    Enterprise
    By request only. The company asks, Bench staff set the seats and the price, and the company accepts and pays.

  3. Set the brand Ready today

    You
    Choose the company colours, upload the logo, pick a card style, and lock the design if you want every card to look the same.
    Happens
    Every employee card uses the company look.
    Where
    Brand

  4. Invite the team Ready today

    You
    Add people by email, or import a spreadsheet.
    Happens
    Each person gets an invitation, joins, and gets their own card in the company brand. One card per seat.
    Where
    Team

  5. Employees fill in their cards Ready today

    They
    Add their name, title, photo and links.
    Happens
    Fields can be free, needing approval, or locked (approval rules are an Enterprise feature). Employees can use the extras the company allows, such as a shop or booking.
    Where
    Cards

  6. The company card Ready today

    You
    Make one shared company card for the website, the reception, and events.
    Happens
    Contacts that arrive on this card belong to the company. Owners and admins see them, and they can be emailed about each new one.
    Where
    Cards → Company card

  8. When someone leaves Ready today

    You
    Remove the person and choose what happens to their card: keep it, hand it to someone else, or delete it.
    Happens
    A kept card with no holder counts as the company's, so its contacts stay with the company.
    Where
    Team → Remove

    Where
    Plan and billing

Asking for EnterpriseHow a company gets a custom plan

  1. The owner asks Ready today

    You
    On the Billing page, fill in how many seats you need and a short message.

  2. Bench staff reply with a quote Ready today

    Happens
    In the back office, staff set the seats and the price for this company. The owner gets an email in English or Arabic.

  3. The owner accepts and pays Ready today

    Happens
    The Enterprise features switch on and the plan renews yearly like the others. Seats can never be set below the number already in use.
The ideaAn event or exhibition organiser makes Bench the official digital card partner. Every exhibitor, speaker and visitor gets a card in the event's look, and the organiser gets results to show its sponsors. Much of the base already exists. The event-specific tools are planned.

Three ways to do the dealPick one for the first event

1. Official partner

The organiser includes Bench in every exhibitor package and pays Bench per exhibitor or per attendee. Simple for exhibitors.

2. Exhibitor add-on

The organiser sells a "Bench Event Pack" as an optional extra and shares the revenue with Bench. Low risk for the organiser.

3. Sponsor-funded

A sponsor pays, so cards are free for everyone and carry the sponsor's name. Bench earns from the sponsor.

Before the eventSetting everything up

  1. Agree the tie-up Planned (business step)

    Happens
    You and the organiser agree the model, the price, who owns visitor details, and the consent wording. A lawyer should check this for Qatar.

  2. Create the event workspace Ready today

    You
    Make a company workspace for the organiser, with the event's colours and logo and a locked design.
    Happens
    You can pick one of the event-themed page styles (speaker, exhibitor booth, expo hall, awards gala, music festival, art fair).

  3. Add exhibitors and speakers Partly built

    You
    Import the list from a spreadsheet.
    Happens
    Everyone gets a card in the event brand. Today this works through the company team import. A version made for events is planned.

  4. Publish the directory Ready today

    Happens
    The company directory becomes the exhibitor and speaker directory, with search. It is an Enterprise feature. It can sit on the event's own web address once a custom domain is set up (not yet tested on a real domain).

  5. Print the booth QR poster Planned

    Happens
    One click makes a print-ready A4, A3 or roll-up banner with the event or company brand and a big QR: "Scan to get our catalogue and meet our team".

On the dayAt the stand

  1. Visitors scan or tap Ready today

    They
    Scan the stand's QR or tap a staff member's NFC card.
    Happens
    They see the card, save the contact, and can leave their own details with a clear consent line.

  2. Staff capture leads Partly built

    You
    Use Event mode for a full-screen QR and quick "I met someone" notes that save offline. Scan a visitor's paper card to add it.
    Planned
    A kiosk mode (a tablet where visitors type their own details) and Hot / Warm / Cold marks with interest tags.
    Where
    Share → Event and Contacts → Add contact → Scan

  3. Visitors get a follow-up Planned

    Happens
    Right after they leave details, the visitor gets a message with the catalogue link. The staff member is reminded to follow up.

  4. Who owns the leads Ready today

    Happens
    Leads from the company card belong to the company. Leads from a staff member's own card stay private unless that person has turned on sharing. Exhibitor staff can use either.

After the eventProving it worked

  1. Reports Planned

    Exhibitor
    Leads per hour, leads per staff member, and a list of hot leads to call first.
    Organiser
    Cards created, scans, connections made, and the busiest stands, to show sponsors. Basic analytics and export exist today for company cards.

  2. Follow-up and reminders Partly built

    Happens
    Contacts keep the "met at the event" tag, and assigned contacts notify the person. Automatic reminders "You met 40 people, send a hello?" are planned.

  3. From the event to paying customers Ready today

    Happens
    People who liked the card start a Personal plan, and exhibitors turn the event setup into a Company plan for their team.

A small pilot firstHow to prove it with one event

  1. Pick one event

    A small or medium one, with an organiser who already knows you.

  2. Offer it free or at cost

    For 10 to 30 exhibitor cards, a booth QR poster and a speaker directory.

  3. Measure

    Cards created, scans, contacts saved, and how many exhibitors say they would pay next time.

  4. Write a one-page case study

    Use it to sell the next event.
